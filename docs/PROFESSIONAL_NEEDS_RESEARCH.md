# Professional-needs research

Reviewed 17 October 2026. SOPHIA is in development, with restricted beta 0.7.87. We are exploring which information tasks matter to Italian pharmacists, pharmacies, pharmacy networks and other professionals. The research is not a clinical or market validation.

## Current website flow

On the [public website](https://sophia-pharmacy.enricogiuliano.chatgpt.site/), choose **Survey and waitlist** and a role. The current single-screen form combines professional-needs answers and early-access waitlist registration, with one **Submit and join the waitlist** action. Role, at least one priority and email are required. Further information about work difficulties, frequency, current tools, requirements, hypothetical budget, name, organisation, region and a follow-up interview is optional. Participation itself is voluntary.

The server validates the same requirements and records a request only after successful submission. A positive server receipt closes the form, returns to the home page and confirms submission; errors retain the entered answers. This flow does not send an email automatically. Researchers, developers, AI experts and potential partners retain the collaboration/email path. The email alternative prepares a draft for the participant to send; it does not automatically create a dashboard record.

Protocol `sophia-public-2026-10-16-03`, action `survey_and_waitlist`, scope `early_access_needs`. The answers accompany early-access expectations and organisation, without authorising unrelated use. Earlier protocols 01/02 remain accepted for already-open pages. Older survey-only and waitlist-only records retain their original consent and are not reinterpreted as the combined action. See the [current privacy notice](https://sophia-pharmacy.enricogiuliano.chatgpt.site/privacy.html).

Navigation statistics are separate and currently enabled by default, with opt-out in the footer and owner/test browser exclusions. Form submission does not change that setting or automatically join navigation to a named respondent. Historical consent records keep their original scope. The owner-only dashboard separates page views, sessions, needs responses and waitlist records. Earlier uncollected navigation is not reconstructed. These interface facts are not an assertion of complete collection or regulatory conformity.

## Interpreting the findings

This is exploratory, self-selected research, not a representative survey of Italian pharmacists. A submission is not a paid reservation, active app use, verified professional qualification or demonstrated willingness to pay. Budget ranges can influence responses and are not prices or offers. We do not assert findings or market validation in this document.

Do not submit patient data, credentials or confidential third-party information. Any public findings require an appropriately aggregated account without identifiers.

App and website releases are separate: app 0.7.87 is deployed; website publication 49 aligns its status and preserves the combined form. No new market findings or model answers are asserted by this documentation update. See [status and limits](STATUS.md).
