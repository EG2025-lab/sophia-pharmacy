# Professional-needs research

Reviewed 16 October 2026. SOPHIA is in development, with restricted beta 0.7.83. We are exploring which information tasks matter to Italian pharmacists, pharmacies, pharmacy networks and other professionals. The research is not a clinical or market validation.

## Current website flow

On the [public website](https://sophia-pharmacy.enricogiuliano.chatgpt.site/), choose **Join the waitlist** and a role. The current single-screen form combines professional-needs answers and early-access waitlist registration, with one **Submit and join the list** action. Role, at least one priority and email are required. Further information about work difficulties, frequency, current tools, requirements, hypothetical budget, name, organisation, region and a follow-up interview is optional. Participation itself is voluntary.

The server validates the same requirements and records a request only after successful submission. A positive server receipt closes the form, returns to the home page and confirms submission; errors retain the entered answers. This flow does not send an email automatically. Researchers, developers, AI experts and potential partners retain the collaboration/email path. The email alternative prepares a draft for the participant to send; it does not automatically create a dashboard record.

Protocol `sophia-public-2026-10-16-03`, action `survey_and_waitlist`, scope `early_access_needs`. The answers accompany early-access expectations and organisation, without authorising unrelated use. Earlier protocols 01/02 remain accepted for already-open pages. Older survey-only and waitlist-only records retain their original consent and are not reinterpreted as the combined action. See the [current privacy notice](https://sophia-pharmacy.enricogiuliano.chatgpt.site/privacy.html).

Navigation statistics are separate, optional and off without consent. Form submission does not enable them or automatically join page views to a person's answers. The owner's server-protected dashboard separates views, sessions, responses and subscriptions; it measures only consented navigation and does not import Gmail messages. Navigation collection began16 October 2026 at 10:39 Europe/Rome; missing earlier data must not become invented visits or zero visitors. Aggregates cover all submissions in the chosen period; the list/export is limited to the latest1,000 requests. The dashboard is not a public contact database.

## Interpreting the findings

This is exploratory, self-selected research, not a representative survey of Italian pharmacists. A submission is not a paid reservation, active app use, verified professional qualification or demonstrated willingness to pay. Budget ranges can influence responses and are not prices or offers. We do not assert findings or market validation in this document.

Do not submit patient data, credentials or confidential third-party information. Any public findings require an appropriately aggregated account without identifiers.

App and website releases are separate: app 0.7.83 is deployed with an offline-qualified documentary-privacy repair; website publication 33 preserves the combined form introduced in 32. This documentation alignment changes neither the app engine nor maturity scores. See [status and limits](STATUS.md).
