# Professional-needs research

14 October 2026. SOPHIA is in development. We are exploring which information tasks matter to Italian pharmacists, pharmacies, pharmacy networks and other professionals, and what would make an AI tool useful enough to consider using or paying for.

On the [public project website](https://sophia-pharmacy.enricogiuliano.chatgpt.site/), choose **Join the waitlist**, then your role. The four professional roles open an optional three-step survey:

1. Tasks to simplify and a recurring work difficulty.
2. Search frequency, time spent and current AI use.
3. Desired requirements and a hypothetical monthly budget, with the unit stated per professional or pharmacy location.

All questions may be skipped. Sharing survey answers and joining the waitlist are independent choices, with separate, unchecked consent controls. Volunteering for a follow-up interview is optional. Developers, AI experts, researchers and potential partners can continue to the existing collaboration form with their chosen field selected.

The website prepares an email draft in Gmail or another email client. Participants review and send it themselves. Answers and requests are received only when the email arrives. The website does not automatically send messages, store survey responses in a database, or call an AI model. See the [privacy notice](https://sophia-pharmacy.enricogiuliano.chatgpt.site/privacy.html).

## Interpreting the findings

This is exploratory, self-selected research, not a representative survey of all Italian pharmacists. A submitted answer is not a paid reservation, an active user, a product qualification or demonstrated willingness to pay. The displayed budget ranges can influence responses and are not SOPHIA prices or offers. Findings should be interpreted alongside concrete tasks, existing alternatives and follow-up interviews.

Any public findings will be aggregated without personal identifiers. Do not include patient data, confidential third-party information or credentials. There are no survey findings or market-validation claims in this document yet.

The survey concerns product discovery. The restricted app remains version 0.7.80 with the separately documented limitations; this website update does not fix the app, promote candidate 0.7.81 or change its evaluation scores.
