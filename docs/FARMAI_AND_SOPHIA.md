The plugin should attempt its relevant operation first. Recoverable source failures can lead to actual web reading with a declared channel and limitations. A found link alone is not a document read. A clinical receipt summary is not proof that every field of its original raw ledger was rendered identically.

Technical migration does not establish clinical validation, general repeatability or broad counter-use readiness. Existing access restrictions and source limitations remain binding. [Current SOPHIA status](STATUS.md).
